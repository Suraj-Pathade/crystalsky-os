## Overview

Welcome to **CrystalSky OS** — your complete, production-ready Photography & Film Business Management System built specifically to eliminate forgotten payments, track event expenses, manage freelancer rates, calculate event profitability, and sync Google Calendar & WhatsApp notifications.

---

## Key Workflows & Features

### 1. Recording Your First Real Client & Event Booking
1. Click **+ Quick Add** (or **+ New Event**).
2. Select an existing client or choose **"+ Create New Client"** (e.g. Client Name & Phone).
3. Enter shoot details: Event Name (e.g. *Rahul & Sneha Wedding*), Event Type, Date, Venue, City, and Google Maps location link.
4. Enter agreed **Total Contract Value** (e.g. ₹1,00,000).
5. Click **Save Event Booking**.

---

### 2. Client Payment Tracking & Receipt Generation
1. Go to **Payments** (or click **Record Payment**).
2. Select the shoot booking and enter the amount received (e.g. Advance ₹25,000 or Wedding Day Payment ₹50,000).
3. Select Payment Method (UPI, Cash, Bank Transfer, GPay, PhonePe) and enter Txn Ref.
4. Click **Save Payment**.
5. An instant **Payment Receipt** modal pops up with 2 quick options:
   - **WhatsApp Receipt**: Sends an auto-formatted receipt message to your client via WhatsApp.
   - **Print Receipt**: Generates a clean printable/PDF receipt.

---

### 3. Monthly Expense Tracking (Core Module)
1. Go to **Monthly Expenses**.
2. Select month (e.g. `2026-08`).
3. Click **+ Log Expense**.
4. Select category: *Photographer, Videographer, Candid, Cinematographer, Reel, Drone, Photo/Video Editing, Album Designing, Album Printing, Travel, Fuel, Food, Equipment Rental, Software, Marketing, Misc*.
5. Optionally link the expense to an Event ID so the system automatically computes **Event Profitability**!
6. Export monthly logs to CSV anytime.

---

### 4. Freelancers, Team Assignment & Payables Ledger
1. Go to **Freelancers & Team** to add photographers, videographers, editors, and album printers.
2. In the **Event Detail** page, click **+ Assign Team Member**.
3. Specify their agreed fee for that specific shoot (rates are never fixed).
4. Go to **Team Payables** ("Money I Have To Pay") to see all pending freelancer balances. Record partial or full payouts with 1 click.

---

### 5. Client Receivables & One-Click WhatsApp Payment Reminders
1. Go to **Client Receivables** ("Money I Have To Receive").
2. See all pending balances categorized by **OVERDUE**, **DUE TODAY**, and **DUE SOON**.
3. Click **Send Reminder** next to any overdue client to generate a personalized WhatsApp message with total contract value, paid amount, and pending balance.

---

### 6. Deliverables & Production Workflow
1. In **Deliverables**, track *Photos, Video, Reel, Album Design, and Album Printing*.
2. Mark status from *NOT STARTED* → *IN PROGRESS* → *READY* → *DELIVERED*.
3. When Album & Video are marked **DELIVERED** with a pending balance, a high-priority **FINAL PAYMENT PENDING** alert is triggered with 1-click WhatsApp final payment reminder!

---

### 7. Monthly P&L Reports & Cash Flow
1. Go to **P&L Reports**.
2. Select any month to view **Total Money Received**, **Total Expenses Paid**, **Cash Profit**, **Contract Profit**, and **Margin %**.
3. View the **Event Profitability Matrix** to see which shoots yielded highest profit margin.
