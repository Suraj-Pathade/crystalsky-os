## ✏️ Part 4: How to Edit Shoots, Albums & Tasks Anytime

1. **Editing Shoot Details (Price, Date, Venue)**:
   - Click **`✏️ Edit`** on any shoot card in **Shoots & Bookings** or **Event Detail page**.
   - Update Date via Calendar, update Package Price, or change venue anytime.

2. **Editing Deliverables & Album Status**:
   - Open **Deliverables & Albums**.
   - Click **`✏️ Edit`** on any deliverable to change status (`IN PROGRESS`, `ALBUM DESIGN`, `PRINTING`, `DELIVERED`) or add Google Drive Album link!

3. **Editing Tasks & To-Dos**:
   - Open **Tasks & To-Dos**.
   - Click **`✏️ Edit`** to update task title, assigned editor/photographer, priority, or due date.
